Part 1 · A few facts, so you are not guessing blind

These are rough figures, and some are a few years old. Use them as anchors, not as truth. If you need something not listed here, write it as an assumption on Sheet 1.

United States

  • High labour cost per hour; automation is usually justified on staff time, not on access.
  • Healthcare carries a large administrative and billing workload alongside clinical work.
  • Cloud services and software tooling are widely adopted in most organisations.
  • Sector-specific regulation on data (health, education, finance) is strict.

India

  • 22 scheduled languages; public services must often work across several at once.
  • Large-scale digital public infrastructure for identity and payments is in place.
  • A large share of employment is in agriculture and in very small businesses.
  • Mobile-first usage is the norm; desktop access is not assumed.

Kenya

  • Agriculture and livestock make up over a third of GDP; most farms are smallholdings.
  • Mobile money (M-Pesa) is the dominant payment rail. Around 80% of adults sent or received a digital payment (2017).
  • Roughly 95% of adults had a mobile subscription, but under half had broadband (2018).
  • Swahili and English are both official; extension services reach farmers unevenly.
Figures: Kenya Bureau of Statistics; Communications Authority of Kenya; World Bank.

Part 2 · The autonomy ladder (we will read this together after the discussion)

Autonomy is not on or off. It has steps. Look back at the three tasks you wrote, and ask which step each one needs.

StepWhat the machine doesWhat the human does
1CompletionPredicts the next few words or linesAccepts or rejects, constantly
2ChatAnswers questions, produces textCopies the result across by hand
3CopilotProposes a specific changeApproves each one
4AgentPlans, uses tools, acts, checks its own result, repeatsSets the goal, approves at key points
5AutonomousTakes a request and runs to completionReviews the outcome only
Three conditions, one answer. Repetitive, multi-step, currently done by a person. Those are not arbitrary. They are the conditions under which step 4 becomes worth building. You just identified them yourself, three times, without being told what an agent is.
